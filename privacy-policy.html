Kisan AI — Privacy Policy

Android App package: com.Smart.farmer
Effective date: 26 July 2026  •  Last updated: 26 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kisan AI (किसान AI) mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use it. By installing or using the App you agree to this policy.

In short: You can use the App as a guest without creating an account. We do not sell your personal data. Your farm profile, favourites and AI chat history are stored on your own device. Location is used only to fetch local weather and nearby places.

1. Who we are

The App is published by an independent developer ("we"). For any privacy question or request, contact awarenivrutti2011@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the information described below.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

DataWhenWhy
Mobile numberOnly if you choose phone OTP sign-inTo create and secure your account (via Firebase Authentication)
Google account name, email address, profile photoOnly if you choose Google sign-inTo create your account and show your name in the App
Farm profile: your name, village, district, state, farm size, preferred cropsOnly if you fill in the profile screenTo personalise advisories, prices and notifications
Crop photosOnly when you take or upload a photo for disease detection or AI chatTo generate an AI diagnosis of the crop problem
Questions you type or speak to the AI assistantWhen you use the AI assistantTo generate an answer

Guest mode: you may use the App without signing in. In guest mode we collect no account data at all.

2.2 Information collected automatically

DataPurpose
Approximate or precise location (only with your permission)To show weather, rain and storm alerts, irrigation advice and nearby mandis, shops, veterinary hospitals and agriculture offices for your area. Coordinates are sent to weather and map providers to fetch results.
Device and app diagnostics: device model, OS version, app version, crash logs, anonymous usage eventsTo fix crashes and improve stability and features (Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics)
Firebase installation ID; push token if you enable notificationsTo deliver the weather, price and scheme notifications you opted into
Advertising ID and ad interaction dataTo display ads and measure them (Google AdMob). See section 5.
Purchase status of the "Remove ads" in-app productTo unlock your ad-free upgrade and restore it on reinstall (Google Play Billing). We never receive or store your card or UPI details — Google Play handles all payments.

2.3 What we never collect

  • We do not collect your contacts, SMS messages, call logs or files outside the photos you pick.
  • We do not record audio in the background. Voice input is transcribed by your device's own speech recognition only while you hold the microphone button.
  • We do not collect bank account numbers, card numbers, Aadhaar numbers or other government ID numbers.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the App's features: weather, mandi prices, crop advisory, soil and pest guidance, government scheme information, calculators, maps and the AI assistant.
  • To personalise content to your district and crops.
  • To send only the notification categories you have switched on. You can turn each off at any time in Profile then Notification settings.
  • To diagnose crashes, prevent abuse and improve the App.
  • To show and measure advertising, and to remove advertising if you purchase the upgrade.

We rely on your consent (for location, camera, microphone and notifications), and on our legitimate interest in operating and securing the App.
